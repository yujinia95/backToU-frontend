How it comes back to you

  1. 1

    Report it

    Lost something or found something? Post a short listing. What it is, where, and when.

  2. 2

    We compare listings

    Every lost report is checked against the found board for items that share a category, description, or location.

  3. 3

    You reconnect

    Review the possible matches on your report and reach out to arrange getting it back.
